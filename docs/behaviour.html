Where they break

Proximity Exponent is the knob, and almost nobody reaches for it first. It decides how sharply alarm falls off with distance, and no change to a radius substitutes for it.

Value
1linear. Something at the very edge of its radius is already mildly alarming, so a heavy source builds enough alarm to flee from a long way off
3the default. The halfway point contributes an eighth as much, so you can walk most of the way in
6-8they let you get almost on top of them, then all go at once
Raising Max Notice Radius to make them break sooner is the trap. It widens the species' attention, so distant sources start contributing, but a source at the edge of a linear falloff is already contributing - which is why the flock was breaking too early in the first place. Change the exponent.

Panic Radius (120) is the backstop underneath all of it: inside it a bird flees regardless of what it has accumulated, which is what catches a sprint or a teleport that would otherwise cross the whole falloff in one frame.

Perk and flee

Two thresholds on one accumulator, both 0..1.

PropertyDefault
Perk Threshold0.25alarm before a bird looks up and turns to track
Flee Threshold0.75alarm before it launches
Perk Release Ratio0.6fraction of the perk threshold it must fall back to before relaxing. Below 1 so birds do not flicker
Alert Gain / Decay1.5 / 0.6how fast alarm builds and fades
You wantDo
Birds that watch you the whole way in and then godrop Perk Threshold to 0.1, leave Flee Threshold alone
Birds that go without warningraise Perk Threshold to just under Flee Threshold
Birds that startle and settle againraise Alert Decay to 1.5+ and drop Flee Threshold
Birds that will not be scared off a feedraise Flee Threshold to 0.95 and Max Threat Rate down to 0.8

Ragged, not synchronised

Two things stop a scatter reading as a switch being thrown, and they pull in opposite directions.

PropertyDefault
Threshold Jitter0.25spreads both thresholds across the birds, so they do not reach theirs together
Contagion Strength1.2how hard one bird leaving alarms its flockmates
Contagion Window0.4show long that lasts
Use itDo not
Jitter at 0.25+ on any flock over about six birds, alwaysJitter at 0, unless you specifically want a single startled animal made of many bodies
Contagion high with a short window, for a bang: the whole flock goes inside half a second, but visibly in sequenceContagion at 0 with high jitter, which reads as birds ignoring each other
Contagion low with a long window, for a flock that empties over two or three secondsContagion so high it swamps the jitter, which puts you back at a synchronised launch
Contagion is two floats in the flock's shared fragment, not a neighbour query. It does not fall off with distance and it does not cross between flocks: every bird in the volume feels the same amount. That is why the window is short.

Splitting the scatter

Orbit Preference is the chance a spooked bird wheels overhead rather than heading straight back down. On the volume it overrides the species; leave the volume's at -1 to take the species' value.

Value
0everyone relocates. Reads as the flock giving up the spot
0.5the default, and the one that reads as birds. Half wheel, half go, and the flock visibly splits
1everyone circles and comes back. Reads as the flock waiting you out

A relocating bird looks for a slot at least Safe Relocate Distance (800) from what spooked it, on the far side of itself, within Land Search Radius (3000). Failing that it orbits anyway - so a flock with nowhere safe to go behaves like Orbit Preference 1 whatever you set.

Making a settled flock look alive

Four things fill the time, on four independent intervals. All are deliberately long.

PropertyDefault
Rest Interval Min / Max6-18sa preen, a head cock, a caw. Needs rest breaks authored
Walk Interval Min / Max9-28sa few dawdled steps. Ground birds only; needs a Walk clip
Glance Interval Min / Max2.5-7sa look. Uses the turn clips it already has
Restless Interval Min / Max35-110sgetting up and moving somewhere else, with nothing wrong
Ambient Airborne Fraction0.15how much of the flock it tries to keep in the air at all times
Ambient Airborne Fraction is the one that matters most and is easiest to miss. Left to the restless interval alone, a lap overhead is far too rare to catch: on ten birds it works out at one short orbit every twenty seconds or so. This makes it a standing target, so there is usually something wheeling above a settled flock. Zero switches it off.
Shortening the rest and walk intervals is the wrong instinct when a flock looks static. A bird playing a break every three seconds reads as agitated, not busy. Reach for Ambient Airborne Fraction and more perch slots first; the shuffling is meant to be something you catch out of the corner of your eye.

Landing and staying landed

PropertyDefault
Min Flight Time2sshortest time airborne before a bird will consider coming down
Landed Cooldown3safter touching down, alert decays Refractory Decay Scale (3×) faster and the flee bar is raised by Refractory Flee Bonus (0.15)
Max Orbit Time20swith no slot free for this long, a bird gives up and drops onto open ground

Ping-ponging - land, launch, land - means the thing that spooked them is still there and alert is rebuilding faster than the cooldown sheds it. Raise Landed Cooldown and Refractory Flee Bonus; if the source genuinely never leaves, that flock wants a smaller Max Notice Radius instead.

PropertyDefault

You wantDo
A tight murmurationCohesion Weight up, Jitter Amplitude down, Cruise Radius down
Loose scavengersCohesion Weight to 0.3, Jitter Amplitude to 300+
Gulls riding a thermalAttractor Sweep Degrees to 25, Cruise Speed down, Bank Scale up
Startled pigeonsCruise Speed and Turn Rate Degrees up, Orbit Time short
No bird ever looks at another bird. Cohesion is a pull toward one moving point per flock, which is what makes the whole thing wheel and what makes the cost flat in flock size. The one exception is Separation, which is O(n²) within a chunk and runs at Near only by default - the further out you let it run, the more it costs and the less anyone can see it.

Clip choice in the air follows what the bird is doing: Bank Left/Right past Bank Clip Yaw Rate, else Glide past Glide Descent Rate, else Fly. Both alternatives are optional. Details on Flying.

If a bank clip is only the fly clip with a static roll on it, do not author it. The roll above is continuous, proportional and eases; a clip's is a fixed angle that snaps on and off. Author Bank Left/Right for a genuinely different pose - wings swept, tail fanned - and set Bank Scale to 0 if you do, so the two do not stack.

Then pick a direction

AmbientBirds as atmosphere. Nobody will walk up to them, and the job is to never be noticed as a system. ReactiveBirds the player reads. Their leaving is information, and it has to be legible from a distance.
