Species

One kind of bird: its baked mesh, its clips, its breaks and its tuning. Everything except the per-flock mix, which is on the volume.

Clips

Clip
Idlerequired. Everything falls back to it
Turn Left, Turn Righttracking a threat, and idle glances
Take Off, Fly, Landrequired for a bird to ever leave the ground. Without Fly the flock is permanently earthbound, which is a valid way to ship one
Take Off Loopoptional. Held once Take Off has finished but the bird is still climbing. Unmapped, that gap is Fly
Land Loopoptional. The descent itself, from committing to coming down until the feet are on the ground. Unmapped, the descent is Glide, or Fly if there is no glide either
Glideoptional. Replaces Fly while descending faster than Glide Descent Rate, and stands in for Land Loop when that is unmapped
Bank Left, Bank Rightoptional. Replace Fly while turning harder than Bank Clip Yaw Rate
Walka slow dawdle. Without it birds stand still between idles
Rest breaksauthored separately, below
Take Off Loop only ever plays if Takeoff Time outlasts the Take Off clip. Below that the bird is already flying by the time the one-shot ends, so the bridge goes straight to Fly and the loop is dead data. The default 0.35s launch is shorter than most takeoff animations. Land Loop has no such problem: a descent is always long.

A landing runs Land Loop → Land → Idle. Land Loop covers the whole descent, however long it takes; Land starts the moment the feet are down and plays out on the ground, and only when it finishes does the bird go to idle. So author Land as the arrival itself - the flare, the fold, the settle - not as something that happens on the way down.

Each mapping

PropertyDefault
Animation Index0index into the data asset's Animations - the enabled clips, in bake order
Looponon for Idle, Walk, Fly and the turns; off for Take Off, Land and rest breaks
Random Start Phaseoffon for Idle only, and only honoured where a flock wants scattering: the first idle after spawning, and the first after coming down. Re-entering idle from a rest break keeps continuity instead
Must Completeofffinish before another clip may replace this one. Wanted on the turns; leave off for Idle, which is long enough that waiting for it would delay every reaction
Play Rate1multiplies the bird's own rate for this clip alone. Fixes one clip authored at the wrong speed, or slows a walk to match the ground speed it is driven at, without re-baking
Soundsplayed at the bird when the clip starts, one picked at random
Audio TriggerNonefired on the flock's bed when the clip starts
Sound Delay0how far into the clip both land
Indices count the enabled sequences in data-asset order. A disabled clip is dropped and shifts every later index, and adding animations reorders the list (a multi-select drop sorts alphabetically). Re-check every mapping after any re-bake. The failure is silent: birds animate, with the wrong clips.

Rest breaks

A list, not clip slots, because a numbered slot cannot tell you which one is the caw. Each entry has a Name, which is what the list shows and which nothing reads.

PropertyDefault
Namea label. Shown in the list
Animation Index0the same as any other clip
Weight1likelihood relative to the other breaks. Raise on a preen, drop on a full body shake, and one is common while the other stays a treat
Play Rate1
Mirroredofffor a break authored as two clips: a wing stretch left and right, a head cock either way
Mirror Animation Index0the other side
Mirror Chance0.5which side plays, once this break has won its pick
Sounds, Audio Trigger, Sound Delayas per clip. Both sides of a mirrored pair share them

A mirrored pair is one entry, so it spends one Weight across both sides. Two entries would make it twice as likely as a single-clip break, which is backwards: paired breaks tend to be the big conspicuous ones you wanted rare.

There are 12 playback slots behind the list. Anything past that is dropped with a warning.
Set Sound Delay on a caw. The sound belongs where the beak opens, not on frame 0.

Per-bird variation

PropertyDefault
Scale Min / Max0.95 / 1.05so a flock is not one bird repeated
Play Rate Jitter0.1spread on playback speed
Mesh Yaw Offset0correction, in degrees, for art that does not face +X. Applied everywhere facing comes from a direction, so a bird authored facing +Y flies forwards rather than sideways
Bounds Radius30roughly the bird's radius in world units. Feeds the screen-size estimate that drives LOD
At any distance the scale and play-rate spread do more for a flock than any behaviour setting, because silhouette size and the timing of small movements are all that survives. 0.9/1.15 and 0.15 is a good pass for a distant flock.

Validation

Right-click the species and Validate Data. It reports a mesh that will not load, a missing Idle mapping, a stale pose match table, and a material instance whose frame count disagrees with the bake.
