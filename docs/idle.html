Idling

Four things fill the time between takeoffs, on four independent per-bird intervals. All of them are deliberately long.

Rest breaks

Every Rest Interval Min/Max a settled bird plays one of its rest breaks once and returns to idle, picked by Weight. The preen, the head cock, the caw.

PropertyDefault
Rest Interval Min / Max6-18s
Allow Rest Breakson

Nothing happens if the species mapped none.

Dawdling

Every Walk Interval Min/Max a bird on open ground wanders somewhere between Walk Min Distance and Walk Radius of the spot it spawned on, turning to face where it is going and slowing into the turn rather than crabbing sideways.

PropertyDefault
Walk Interval Min / Max9-28s
Walk Speed45
Walk Radius150furthest a dawdle strays from the spawn spot
Walk Min Distance45shortest a dawdle is worth making, so a bird never takes half a step
Walk Arrive Distance8
Walk Turn Rate Degrees220/show fast it lines up before setting off
Allow Walkingon

Aiming from the spawn spot rather than from where it currently stands is what keeps a bird that dawdles all day from drifting out of its flock.

Walking never traces. A bird holds the height it was placed at, so keep Walk Radius short on uneven ground or birds will walk into a slope.

Needs a Walk clip. A bird holding a perch slot never walks: it would be stepping off a branch and leaving a reserved perch stood empty.

Glances

Every Glance Interval Min/Max, a turn of up to Glance Yaw Degrees using the turn clips it already has, so it needs no extra animation.

Moving unprompted

Birds also move with nothing disturbing them. On a per-bird interval a settled bird takes off and picks a perch or its original ground spot, weighted by Perch Preference - so it sometimes chooses the same kind of place twice rather than ping-ponging.

PropertyDefault
Restless Interval Min / Max35-110s
Restless Orbit Chance0.3chance the move takes a lap overhead first rather than hopping straight across
Perch Preference0.5chance it targets a perch rather than its ground spot. 0 keeps birds on the ground, 1 keeps them fighting over perches
Ambient Airborne Fraction0.15standing target for how much of the flock is in the air with nothing wrong. Zero switches it off
Ambient Airborne Urgency8×how much faster the restless countdown runs while under that target
Allow Restless Moveson
Ambient Airborne Fraction is the setting that matters most here and the easiest to miss. Left to the interval alone, a lap overhead is far too rare to catch: on ten birds it works out at one short orbit every twenty seconds or so. Below the target, restless countdowns run 8× faster and every move is an orbit; once met, everything drops back to the slow interval.

A voluntary move carries no contagion, so one bird shuffling never panics the flock, and it ignores the threat-avoidance filter because it is not fleeing.

What can interrupt what

All four yield instantly to a real alert, and none can interrupt a clip mid-pose. Clips do not blend into each other, so one cut short is a visible snap - which is what Must Complete on a mapping is for.

These intervals are deliberately long, and shortening them is the wrong instinct when a flock looks static. A bird playing a break every three seconds reads as agitated rather than busy. Reach for Ambient Airborne Fraction and more perch slots first.

Idle behaviours run at every LOD tier except Culled, at that tier's stride. Far is included on purpose: a bird part way through a dawdle has to finish it wherever it is.
