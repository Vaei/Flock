Ambient flocks

Birds as atmosphere. Nobody will walk up to them and nobody will think about them, and the job is to never be noticed as a system.

Five moves

  1. Put them out of reach. A roofline, a spire, a wire across the street. Distance hides everything the simulation cannot do.
  2. Keep something in the air. Ambient Airborne Fraction 0.2. A settled flock that is entirely settled reads as a decal.
  3. Make them hard to spook. Proximity Exponent 4-5, Max Notice Radius down. An ambient flock that scatters every time you cross the square stops being ambience.
  4. Slow everything down. Long intervals, low Attractor Sweep Degrees, low Cruise Speed.
  5. Spend nothing. Separation off, Interpolate Frames off, small counts, and let the LOD tiers do their job.

Orbit Preference high is the important one: an ambient flock that is startled should come back. A flock that relocates permanently leaves a hole in the scene where the birds used to be, and nobody puts them back.

Perches carry the whole look

An ambient flock spends almost all of its life sitting down, so what it looks like is what the perch slots look like.

SourceFor
Splinea wire, a rail, a ridge line. Set Spacing to something irregular against the architecture - 85 rather than 100 - or the birds sit on a grid
Socketsa tree or a statue where an artist has already chosen the spots
Boxa flat roof or a ledge. Position Jitter 0.5+, or it reads as a grid

Vary the authored rotation. Slots that all face the same way turn a roofline into a chorus line; birds sitting on a wire face along it, roughly, but not identically.

Scale Min / Max at 0.9/1.15 and Play Rate Jitter at 0.15 do more for a distant flock than any behaviour setting. At that range you are reading silhouette size and the timing of small movements, and nothing else.

Sound is most of it

An ambient flock is heard far more than it is watched. The bed is fed Distance, BirdCount, Alert and AirborneRatio every frame, so a MetaSound can own the whole curve.

Bedthe settled murmur. Drive its level off Alert and AirborneRatio so it goes quiet when they leave, and thin off BirdCount
Rest break soundsthe caw, the call. This is where the character is. Set Sound Delay so it lands where the beak opens
Take Off / Land one-shotswingbeats. Cheap, spatialised, and what turns a distant scatter into an event you hear

Raise the Weight on whichever break carries the sound. A caw at weight 3 among eight breaks is nearly silent; the crow ships at 25. Full detail on Sound and VFX.

What it should cost

Separation offthe only thing here that is not flat in flock size. An ambient flock is never close enough for anyone to see two birds overlap
Interpolate Frames offit is a per-vertex cost paid in the base pass and every shadow pass, and it buys smoothness on a slow wingbeat seen up close - which is the one thing an ambient flock never is
Near Distance in tightdrop it to 1200 if the flock is never approached. Mid runs at half rate, Far at a sixth
Two bone influencesNumBoneInfluences on the data asset. On a small skeleton it is very hard to tell apart and takes a real bite out of the vertex shader

Checklist

  • Something is in the air at all times.
  • Crossing the square does not scatter them.
  • If it does scatter them, they come back.
  • The slots are not a grid and do not all face one way.
  • You can hear them before you can see them.
  • Separation and interpolation both off.
