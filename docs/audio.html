The bed

Fed these every frame, so a MetaSound can own its own mixing.

Parameter
Distancelistener to flock centre. The MetaSound owns the falloff curve - it is handed a distance, not a volume
BirdCountlive birds in the flock
Alertmean alert, 0..1
AirborneRatiofraction currently flying, 0..1

Plus TakeOff and Land triggers per event, so cascades are audible as cascades.

Drive the bed's level off Alert and AirborneRatio so it goes quiet when the flock leaves, and thin it off BirdCount so a flock losing birds thins rather than cutting out. Those two are what stop a bed sounding like a looping ambience laid over the scene.

Per-clip audio

Any clip mapping and any rest break carries Sounds, an Audio Trigger and a Sound Delay.

Soundsplayed at the bird when the clip starts, one picked at random, from the shared one-shot pool
Audio Triggerfired on the flock's bed, so a MetaSound decides what the moment sounds like
Sound Delayhow far into the clip both land

Rest breaks carry their own audio, set on the break rather than on the species, because which sound goes with a caw is a property of the caw. Both sides of a mirrored pair share the entry's list.

Sounds is a list, picked at random per play. One caw asset repeated across a flock is what gives an ambience away, and separate assets do not contend for one asset's concurrency budget the way repeats of a single one do.
Set Sound Delay on a caw. The beak has to open first, and lining the two up by eye is the difference between a bird cawing and a bird miming.

The pool

One Shot Pool Size (Project Settings → Game → Flock, default 8) caps overlapping one-shots. A cascade past it drops the surplus rather than queueing it: a late caw is worse than a missing one.

Enable One Shot Audio turns them off entirely, leaving the bed and its triggers.

If caws still cut each other off, check Sound Concurrency on the asset. A group with Max Count 1 and Stop Oldest steals the voice however many pool slots are free.

Raise the Weight on whichever break carries the sound. A caw at weight 3 among eight breaks is nearly silent; the crow ships at 25, because crows caw constantly.

Why none of it is spawned from a processor

A Mass processor is not guaranteed to be on the game thread, and spawning an actor, touching a component or broadcasting a delegate from a worker crashes. So events go into a locked queue drained once per frame on the game thread. The per-bird debug draw takes the same route, since DrawDebug* is game thread only.
