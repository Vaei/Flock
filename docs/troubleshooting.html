If it is wrong

Symptom to cause. Watch LogAnimToTextureEditor during a bake - the plugin's own message log listing is never written to - and LogFlock everywhere else.

Reach for these first

flock.Debug.Perception 1    bird state, alert level, and the clip and frame it is playing
flock.Debug.Perception 2    also threat sources
flock.Debug.Perception 3    also flock bounds, the attractor and blocking volumes in use
flock.Debug.Slots 1         perch slots: green free, yellow reserved, red occupied
flock.PoseMatch 0           clips open on their first frame, to A/B the pose match table
stat flock                  Anim and Render non-zero is the proof a flock is live

Right-click the species → Validate Data reports a bad mesh, a missing Idle, a stale pose match table and a material frame-count mismatch in one go.

Nothing renders

SymptomCause
No birds at allNo valid Idle mapping, or the mesh failed to load. Both log to LogFlock
Birds but no animationstat flock shows Anim at zero, so the processors are not running. See Cost
Frozen on the bind poseNumCustomDataFloats disagrees with AutoPlay. Set bAutoPlay on the data asset, never on the material instance - the bake overwrites it
Nothing anywhere, no logEnable Flock off in Project Settings, or a dedicated server

Wrong animation

SymptomCause
Wrong clip playsAn Animations index taken from your source list rather than the enabled clips. flock.Debug.Perception 1 names the clip on the bird
Only the newest clips are frozen, older ones fineThe material instance was not in Bone Material Instances for the re-bake, so its NumFrames still describes the old texture. Everything past it clamps to the last row
Frozen mid-air on one poseA Take Off or Land one-shot that has run out, with no Fly mapped to hand over to
Every bird moves identicallyRandom Start Phase off on the Idle mapping
Clip changes snapNo pose match table, or it went stale on a re-bake and is being ignored. Flock → Build Pose Match Table
A clip opens on a pose unrelated to the one it replacedThe table was built with Velocity Weight at zero, so it matched the shape and not the direction
Still steppy with Interpolate onOnly one of the two halves is set. The material instance needs Interpolate and the species needs Interpolate Frames
Birds fly or walk sidewaysMesh Yaw Offset does not match art that faces something other than +X
One wrong frame per loopFrame offset past the clip's end, sampling the next clip's first frame. See Driving playback yourself
Playback is noisesRGB, mips or compression changed on a baked texture. The bake sets these; leave them
Birds pop at screen edgesMissing bounds extensions on the static mesh

Wrong place

SymptomCause
Birds standing in the airNothing under the volume to trace against, or the ground is steeper than Min Ground Normal Z. LogFlock warns
Birds float or sinkSnap To Ground off puts them on the plane through the volume's centre, not its floor
Birds fly through a buildingNo Flock Blocking Volume around it, or it is further from the flock centre than six others - only that many are kept per flock. flock.Debug.Perception 3 draws the ones in use
Birds hover against a wallA perch slot or the flock volume is inside a blocker, so they are being sent where they cannot go
Birds never finish landing, stuck on the descent clipA blocker's Avoid Margin reaches over the approach. It extends past every face, so a volume that visibly clears the perch can still cover the path down to it
Birds walk into a slopeWalking never traces. Shorten Walk Radius on uneven ground
Perch rebuild does nothingThe perch is inside a Blueprint. Bake All Perches in Level only rebuilds placed instances
Birds never use the perchesEvery slot is already occupied. Author more slots than birds

SymptomCause
They break from far too far awayProximity Exponent too low, not a radius. See Tuning behaviour
The whole flock launches on one frameThreshold Jitter at zero, or Contagion Strength high enough to swamp it
They leave one at a timeContagion Strength too low, or the window too short

They ping-pong: land, launch, landRaise Landed Cooldown and Refractory Flee Bonus. If the source never leaves, lower Max Notice Radius
They circle forever and never settleNo free slots within Land Search Radius. They give up after Max Orbit Time and land on open ground
Nothing is ever in the airAmbient Airborne Fraction at zero, or no Fly clip mapped
A settled flock looks frozenNo rest breaks authored, no Walk clip, or Ambient Airborne Fraction too low

Caws cut each other offSound Concurrency on the asset steals the voice however many pool slots are free

The bake

SymptomCause
"Already used by LightMap"Lightmap index equals the data asset's UVChannel. Move the lightmap to 2
Prepare refuses, naming sequencesThey are on the data asset but not in the recipe. Add them to Anim Sequences, in the order they already sit in, so no index moves
Animation runs past its endSampleRate is not the source clips' frame rate. It is a time step, not a frame count
Jitter or stair-stepping in slow motion8-bit precision quantises position to 256 steps across the whole animation bounds. Use 16-bit
"Too many Bones"Over 256 bones at 8-bit precision. Use 16-bit, which has no bone limit beyond texture width
Stray vertices spikeMore than 4 influences per vertex. Only 4 reach the texture, and the reduction can overflow
Squash and stretch lostBone mode discards bone scale. Use Vertex mode
Root offset ignoredRootTransform is Vertex mode only
Wrong UVs sampledUVChannel of 4+. Only 0-3 work
Mesh conversion returns nullHeadless without -AllowCommandletRendering, or LODIndex left at -1
Changes revert on restartThe bake only marks packages dirty. Leave Save After Bake on
Instance material gets no parametersIt is not on the static mesh asset's slot, or it is a dynamic instance. Only UMaterialInstanceConstant on the asset is reached
Bake succeeds, textures unchangedNull position or rotation texture. The write result is ignored
Editor crashes mid-bakeNull BoneWeightTexture. It hits a bare check()

Known limits

  • Morph targets are not baked in either mode.
  • There is no root motion extraction: move the instance transform yourself.
  • Birds have no collision of any kind. Blocking volumes are the whole of their world awareness.
  • Nothing is replicated, and nothing runs on a dedicated server.
  • A crash in DebugSetProcessor on tick means a FMassRuntimePipeline held without UPROPERTY.
